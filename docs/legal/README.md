# Legal page drafts

`privacy-policy.draft.txt` and `terms-of-service.draft.txt` are **starter drafts**, written from what the
platform actually collects and does. They are not published anywhere and are not legal advice.

1. Have your legal counsel review them and fill in every `[PLACEHOLDER]`.
2. Platform owner → **Software Settings → Legal pages**: paste each text into its box and save.
   `/privacy` and `/terms` go live (and appear in the site footer) within a minute.

Format the pages understand: blank line between paragraphs, `# ` heading, `## ` sub-heading, `- ` bullet lines.
Limit: 20,000 characters each.
