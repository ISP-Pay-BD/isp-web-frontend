# Design brief: Customer self-care dashboard

1. Product and job: residential/business subscriber; the job is "am I online, what do I owe, can I pay or get help"
2. Design read: calm self-service home; ISP portal tokens; plan status first, then money and support
3. References: admin dashboard attention strip; Stripe customer portal billing summary; Linear issue home density
4. Existing system to honor: CustomerPageShell, Satoshi, shadcn Card, NumberFlow sparingly, primary `#f75803`
5. Type: Satoshi; avoid font-black display shouting; tabular-nums for ৳
6. Color: primary for pay CTA; emerald only for online health; muted for secondary links — no rainbow KPI borders
7. Shape: one plan/status composition, then a summary strip (not 4 equal hover-lift tiles)
8. Spacing and density: readable on mobile; strip then charts/lists
9. Motion: CSS only; no Framer; no icon scale on hover
10. Signature: next bill + connection + tickets + paid as a single strip with links
11. Platform conventions: clear pay path; emergency contacts without decorative lift chrome
12. Deliberately not doing: 4 equal KPI cards with top accent bars, purple plan glow, ping chart decorations
