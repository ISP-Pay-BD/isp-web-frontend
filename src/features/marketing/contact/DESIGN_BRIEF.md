# Design brief: Marketing contact page

1. Product and job: ISP owner wanting a demo or sales reply; one job is "send a clear inquiry"
2. Design read: marketing contact form for ISP buyers, language calm/operational, leaning ISP dark landing
3. References: landing ContactSection in this app; Stripe contact (focused form + address); Linear contact (sparse)
4. Existing system to honor: landing tokens `#0c0118` / `#f75803`, Plus Jakarta + Inter, ContactSection, MarketingLayout
5. Type: Plus Jakarta display + Inter body; body ~16px
6. Color: dominant `#0c0118`, neutral white/opacity ramp, accent CTA orange, semantic emerald success only
7. Shape: rounded-2xl containers, controls rounded-xl; elevation via border tint not heavy shadow stack
8. Spacing and density: 8px grid, balanced marketing spacing
9. Motion: one Reveal on title block; form feedback instant; reduced motion = no transform
10. Signature: Dhaka ops contact strip + inquiry radios (Demo / Feature / Enterprise)
11. Platform conventions: web URL `/contact`, keyboard form, hash `#contact-form` for deep link
12. Deliberately not doing: map embeds, KPI tiles, purple glow cards, second accent
